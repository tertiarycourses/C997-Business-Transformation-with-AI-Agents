# Hands-On Labs — Business Transformation with AI Agents

**Course Code:** C997  |  **Conducted by:** Tertiary Infotech Academy Pte Ltd  |  **Version v1.0 · 2 October 2026**

18 hands-on labs across 3 topics. Full step-by-step instructions are in each lab file and in the Learner Guide; the slide deck stays visual and carries only each lab's objective, deliverable and verification step.

## Responsible use

All labs use generative AI tools on their free tiers with your own account. Do not enter confidential, personal or client-identifying data into any public generative AI tool. Treat every AI output as a draft and verify it before relying on it.

## Learning outcomes

- LO1: Evaluate and investigate generative AI business strategies to identify viable opportunities compatible with organisational objectives.
- LO2: Develop action plans to implement business innovation with generative AI and design digital architectures to apply technologies across the business.
- LO3: Manage business innovation and facilitate information flow among stakeholders to review success and develop innovative ideas.

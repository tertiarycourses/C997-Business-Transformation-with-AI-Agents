# Business Transformation with AI Agents

Pick one business idea and carry it end to end with AI: research it, score it, model it, design its
digital architecture, produce a marketing video and pitch deck for it, govern it — and finally automate
the whole pipeline as an agentic AI workflow with a human review gate.

## About the course

This hands-on course shows business owners, managers and professionals how to drive business
transformation with generative AI and AI agents. You choose **one business idea** on Day 1 and take it
through **18 connected labs** — each lab consumes the artifact the previous one produced — ending with a
capstone that automates the entire pipeline as an agentic workflow with human review.

```
Research → Evaluate → Design Thinking → Business Model Canvas → Architecture
   → Script → Voiceover → Soundtrack → Video → Pitch deck
      → Risk → Guardrails & Ethics → Action plan → KPIs → Comms → CAPSTONE (agentic workflow)
```

## Learning outcomes

1. Evaluate and investigate generative AI business strategies to identify viable opportunities compatible with organisational objectives.
2. Develop action plans to implement business innovation with generative AI and design digital architectures to apply technologies across the business.
3. Manage business innovation and facilitate information flow among stakeholders to review success and develop innovative ideas.

## Topics covered

1. **Introduction to Business Innovation and Generative AI** — predictive vs generative AI, the tool landscape, business value levers, investigating and scoring opportunities.
2. **Designing Digital Architectures and Processes for Business Innovation** — Design Thinking, the Business Model Canvas, five-layer digital architecture, the AI content production pipeline, action planning.
3. **Implementing and Managing Business Innovation with Generative AI Tools** — implementation best practices, legal/ethical/security issues, Singapore AI governance, KPIs, stakeholder communication, agentic automation.
